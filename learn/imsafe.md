# IMSAFE 🩺

## What it is

A six-item self-check run before you take responsibility for anything that can hurt
people: **I**llness · **M**edication · **S**tress · **A**lcohol · **F**atigue ·
**E**motion.

The point is not to pass it. The point is that the person most likely to fail on a given
day is the one running the check, and no other part of the system inspects them.

## Origin & evidence

An FAA personal-minimums checklist, taught in the Risk Management Handbook and the Pilot's
Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge as part of aeronautical decision-making. The FAA's own
framing is worth keeping: the important distinction is *"the difference between what is
'legal' in terms of the regulations, and what is 'smart' or 'safe.'"*

> Federal Aviation Administration (2009). *Risk Management Handbook*, FAA-H-8083-2,
> Figure 3-3. https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/handbooks_manuals/aviation/phak

The handbook's own wording:

| | Ask |
| :--- | :--- |
| **I**llness | Do I have any symptoms? |
| **M**edication | Have I been taking prescription or over-the-counter drugs? |
| **S**tress | Am I under psychological pressure from the job? Worried about financial matters, health problems, or family discord? |
| **A**lcohol | Have I been drinking within 8 hours? Within 24 hours? |
| **F**atigue | Am I tired and not adequately rested? |
| **E**motion | Am I emotionally upset? |

Note that the eight-hour rule belongs to **alcohol** - it is the aviation bottle-to-throttle
limit, not a fatigue limit. Fatigue thresholds are a separate question with their own
evidence; see [Fatigue and Performance](./fatigue-and-performance.md).

## How to use it

Run it before taking the pager, before taking command of an incident, and again before any
irreversible action late in a long night. It takes fifteen seconds.

A failed item is not a confession, it is a scheduling fact. The useful output is "I need
relief in an hour", said early, rather than a heroic silence followed by a mistake.

## Worked example

You are paged at 03:00 for the second night running, having gone to bed at midnight with a
cold. Illness, fatigue, and probably emotion are all amber. Nothing here says do not
respond - it says respond, and in the same message say that you are the wrong person to
run an irreversible change tonight, so somebody wakes up now rather than at 05:00.

## Limits

It is self-report, and the conditions it screens for are exactly the ones that degrade
self-assessment - fatigue and stress most of all. It works far better as a checklist you
answer out loud to another person than as a private thought, and it does not replace a
team that is willing to say you look wrecked.
